## 2026-06-26 - Reusable Form ARIA Patterns
**Learning:** Tailwind's `sr-only` class is excellent for adding screen-reader specific context (like 'Required' alongside a visual asterisk) without custom CSS. Toggle button groups benefit greatly from `role="group"`, `aria-labelledby`, and `aria-pressed` properties to mirror radio button semantics.
**Action:** Use these patterns for custom form elements and toggles to ensure high keyboard and screen-reader accessibility.
